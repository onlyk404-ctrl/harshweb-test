Cadence

A website built for a seed round — clear story, fast pages and a demo that sells itself.

Client

Cadence

Services

Website Design,
Development, Motion

Type

Marketing Website

Introduction

Cadence was raising a seed round with a product most investors had never seen. The old site explained the technology; it never explained why anyone should care.

I rewrote the story before touching a single pixel, then built a site that gets to the point in ten seconds and feels like the product it is selling.

Goals

  • Explain the product in the first screen
  • Convert founders, investors and early customers
  • Collect waitlist signups
  • Load in under one second on 4G
  • Look premium without looking generic

Target audience

  • Seed and Series A investors
  • Technical founders evaluating the tool
  • Early adopters joining the waitlist

Approach

Narrative first. I mapped the site to the questions people actually ask — what is it, who is it for, why now, what does it cost me — and cut everything that answered none of them.

Motion with a job to do: every animation either explains the product or reinforces hierarchy. No decoration for its own sake.

A strict performance budget kept the site under 1s on 4G, which matters more than any effect when an investor opens it on a phone.

Design

Oversized editorial typography with a two-colour system

Scroll-linked product demo instead of a static screenshot

A waitlist flow with server-side validation

Deliverables

  • Positioning and website copy
  • Six responsive page designs
  • Front-end build with motion system
  • CMS setup for the team
  • Analytics, SEO and handover
