Brew & Co.

A coffee store that sells subscriptions while the shop is closed.

Client

Brew & Co.

Services

E-commerce Build,
Conversion Optimisation

Type

Online Store

Introduction

Brew & Co. had great coffee and a website that leaked customers at checkout. Mobile traffic was 78% of visits and 22% of orders.

I rebuilt the store around the mobile buyer: fewer steps, faster pages, and a subscription option placed exactly where the decision happens.

Goals

  • Make checkout work on a phone in one hand
  • Turn one-time buyers into subscribers
  • Get product pages under a second
  • Rank for local coffee searches
  • Give the team control without a developer

Target audience

  • Home brewers buying monthly
  • Local customers ordering for pickup
  • Gift buyers who need it fast and simple

Approach

I recorded real sessions and found three drop-off points: shipping costs appearing late, a four-step checkout, and product pages that loaded after people had scrolled.

Subscriptions on the product card — not buried on a second page — moved recurring revenue from an afterthought to the default choice.

Everything is editable by the shop team: collections, bundles, banners and email flows.

Design

Warm, editorial product pages with real photography

One-page checkout with costs visible from the start

Bundle builder for grind, size and roast

Deliverables

  • Store design and theme build
  • Checkout and subscription setup
  • Product photography direction
  • SEO, analytics and email flows
  • Team training and documentation

Stack
