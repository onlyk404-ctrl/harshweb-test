[ Testimonials ]

I'm Harsh, a web developer who cares about the details most people never notice.

[ My start ]

My journey started with a curiosity about how things work on the internet. One broken HTML page turned into a stack of side projects, and those turned into real products for real people.

[ How I work ]

I work end to end — design in Figma, build in React and Next.js, ship to Vercel, and wire up AI where it genuinely helps. Small teams, fast feedback, no hand-off gaps.

[ About me ]

Outside of code, you'll find me sketching interfaces, testing new AI tools before anyone asks, and collecting way too many fonts I'll never use.

Today my focus is clear: websites, apps and AI for companies that want design to work as hard as it looks good.

[ What I do best ]

Web Development, Next.js, React, Node.js, UI/UX, Landing Pages, SaaS, Mobile Apps, React Native, AI Integration, LLM Apps, Automation, REST APIs, Headless CMS, Performance, SEO.

Over the years I moved from building simple sites to shipping full products — dashboards, mobile apps and AI features that people rely on daily.

I like working with companies that have a strong point of view and want their product to feel considered, fast and effortless.

Currently based in India, working with teams worldwide.
